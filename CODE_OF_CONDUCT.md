# Code of Conduct

Flashlight contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect differing experience levels, identities, equipment budgets, and creative workflows.
- Protect private information found in logs, recordings, screenshots, and library paths.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related
community spaces.
